# Evaluation Rubric

Score every dimension from **0 to 5**.

| Score | General meaning |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Excellent; no material issue |
| 4 | Strong; minor issue only |
| 3 | Adequate; noticeable limitation |
| 2 | Weak; material problem |
| 1 | Very poor; major failure |
| 0 | Fails the dimension completely / unusable |

## Accuracy — 30%

**5:** Claims are correct and appropriately qualified.

**3:** Mostly correct but contains a minor error, omission, or imprecise claim.

**1:** Contains a major factual error that materially changes the answer.

**0:** Fundamentally false or unusable.

## Reasoning — 20%

Assess whether the reasoning is logically coherent, appropriately sequenced, and supported.

Do not reward length. A concise valid explanation can score higher than a long but circular explanation.

## Relevance — 15%

Does the response directly address the user's actual question without unnecessary digression?

## Completeness — 15%

Does it cover the important parts required by the prompt, including explicit sub-questions and constraints?

## Instruction Following — 10%

Did it follow format, scope, audience, language, length, constraints and other explicit instructions?

## Clarity — 10%

Is the response understandable, well organized and appropriately precise?

## Pairwise preference

After independent scoring, select:

- **A preferred**
- **B preferred**
- **Tie / effectively equivalent**
- **Neither / both materially deficient**

The pairwise decision must be based on the rubric and prompt intent, not personal writing preference.

## Severity

Use:

- **Low:** cosmetic or non-material issue
- **Medium:** meaningful limitation but answer remains usable
- **High:** materially misleading, unsafe, or incorrect answer
- **Critical:** answer is fundamentally unusable or could cause serious harm if acted upon

Severity is separate from score.
